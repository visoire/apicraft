# Code of Conduct

Be kind and be useful. This tracker is for people who build things and want a small API to work well.

- Be respectful. No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Stay on topic and assume good intent. Bad news can be said politely.
- Do not post other people's private information, and never post keys, tokens or passwords.
- No spam, advertising or unrelated promotion.

Comments that break these rules may be edited or removed, and repeat offenders may be blocked from the repository. Report a problem with a comment by contacting the maintainer, [visoire](https://github.com/visoire).
